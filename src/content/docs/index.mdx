Welcome to my Minecraft development documentation! Here you'll find mods, datapacks, and other resources I've created for Modded Minecraft. Whether you're a seasoned modder or just getting started, I hope you find something useful here.

:::note[Server-Side Mods]
If a mod has a <Badge text="S" variant="tip" /> badge, it means it's a server-side mod and does not need to be installed on the client unless required by a dependency. Just put it in your server's `mods` folder and you're good to go!
:::

Mods are listed in the sidebar on the left. Each mod has its own documentation with installation instructions, features, and more.
