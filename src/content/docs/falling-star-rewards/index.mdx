A Server-side mod that adds falling stars to the world, which can drop a variety of items where they land. The mod is highly configurable, allowing server owners to customize the behavior and rewards of falling star events.

## Dependencies

- [Matthiesen Lib API](https://modrinth.com/mod/matthiesen-lib-api/)
