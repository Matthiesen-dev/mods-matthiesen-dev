Cobblemon Move Tutor adds the following commands to the game:

## `/move-tutor`

Opens the Move Tutor's GUI, allowing you to browse and learn new moves for your Pokemon.

- **Required Permission:** `cobblemon_move_tutor.command.move_tutor`

## `/move-tutor other <player>`

Opens the Move Tutor's GUI for another player, allowing you to browse and learn new moves for their Pokemon.

- **Required Permission:** `cobblemon_move_tutor.command.move_tutor.other`
